# Scrap Adda

### Smart India Hackathon 2026 — Scrap & E-Waste Management Platform

Scrap Adda is a digital platform concept designed to connect informal scrap collectors, aggregators, and authorized recyclers through a more transparent and traceable digital workflow.

The project focuses on digitizing the journey from scrap collection to aggregation, valuation, recycler matching, transaction, payment, and traceability.

---

## Overview

The existing scrap ecosystem is highly fragmented. Informal collectors and aggregators often operate with limited price visibility, limited access to authorized recyclers, and limited digital records of transactions.

Scrap Adda proposes a collector-first digital platform that connects:

- Kabadiwalas / Waste Pickers
- Aggregators
- Authorized Recyclers
- Government / Regulatory stakeholders

The proposed workflow transforms an informal scrap transaction into a structured digital process.
